# Code of Conduct

## Our pledge

We want taking part in this project to be a harassment-free experience for everyone, whatever their age, body size, disability, ethnicity, sex characteristics, gender identity and expression, level of experience, education, socio-economic status, nationality, personal appearance, race, religion, or sexual identity and orientation.

We pledge to act in ways that make the community open, welcoming, diverse, inclusive and healthy.

## Our standards

Behaviour that helps:

- Showing empathy and kindness towards other people
- Respecting opinions, viewpoints and experiences that differ from your own
- Giving feedback constructively and accepting it gracefully
- Taking responsibility for mistakes, apologising to those affected, and learning from them
- Focusing on what is best for the community as a whole

Behaviour that is not acceptable:

- Sexualised language or imagery, and sexual attention or advances of any kind
- Trolling, insulting or derogatory comments, and personal or political attacks
- Public or private harassment
- Publishing someone else's private information, such as a physical or email address, without their explicit permission
- Other conduct that could reasonably be considered inappropriate in a professional setting

## Enforcement responsibilities

The project maintainer is responsible for clarifying and enforcing these standards, and will take appropriate and fair corrective action in response to behaviour they consider inappropriate, threatening, offensive or harmful. That can include removing or editing comments, commits, code, issues and other contributions, and will be explained where appropriate.

## Scope

This Code of Conduct applies in all project spaces, and when someone is officially representing the project in public.

## Reporting

Report abusive, harassing or otherwise unacceptable behaviour to the maintainer privately, through the "Report content" option on the relevant GitHub comment or by contacting [@joecrockett5](https://github.com/joecrockett5) on GitHub. All complaints will be reviewed and investigated promptly and fairly, and the privacy of anyone who reports an incident will be respected.

## Enforcement guidelines

1. **Correction.** For a minor lapse: a private note explaining what was inappropriate and why.
2. **Warning.** For a single incident or a pattern: a warning with consequences for continued behaviour.
3. **Temporary ban.** For a serious violation: a temporary ban from interacting with the project.
4. **Permanent ban.** For a pattern of violations, harassment of an individual, or aggression towards or disparagement of groups of people: a permanent ban.
